Privacy

Last updated: 24 September 2026. AL CUTS processes personal information in line with South Africa’s Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA).

1. Who we are

AL CUTS (“we”) is a barbershop at 12 Oxford Road, Rosebank, Johannesburg. For POPIA purposes we are the responsible party for booking information you give us. Contact: book@alcuts.co.za · 011 555 0142.

2. What data we collect

  • Booking details: service, barber, date, time, and optional notes.
  • Contact details: your full name, email address and phone number.
  • Technical minimum: whether you have seen the first-visit offer (a session flag) so the popup opens once per visit.

We do not collect ID numbers, banking details or passwords on this website — payment happens at the chair.

3. Why we use it

We use your details only to confirm and manage your visit, contact you about changes, apply the first-visit offer, and run the shop schedule. We will not use your number or email for marketing unless you clearly opt in at the shop.

4. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Booking details are visible only to the shop’s barbers and only as needed to honour your appointment. If the law requires disclosure (for example a court order), we will disclose only what is required.

5. How long we keep it

Confirmed booking records are kept for 24 months for schedule, tax and dispute purposes, then deleted or de-identified. Session flags expire when you close the browser. On this demonstration build, bookings are stored in your own browser (local storage) rather than on our server — clearing your browser storage removes them from your device.

6. Your rights and deletion

Under POPIA you may ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, object to processing, or request deletion. Email book@alcuts.co.za with “POPIA request” in the subject line and we will respond within a reasonable time. You may also lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa.

7. Security

We apply reasonable safeguards: access limited to the barbers who need it, devices kept updated, and no more data collected than the booking needs. No method is perfectly secure, so please do not send sensitive documents through the booking form.

8. Children’s data

Kids-cut bookings include a parent or guardian’s contact details, provided by that adult with consent. We do not knowingly collect information directly from children.

9. Contact

Questions about this notice: AL CUTS, 12 Oxford Road, Rosebank, Johannesburg · book@alcuts.co.za · 011 555 0142
