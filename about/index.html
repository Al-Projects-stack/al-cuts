The story

Al Mujati opened AL CUTS on Oxford Road in 2014 with a single chair and a simple rule: the detail is the whole job. Eleven years on, the shop runs three chairs in Rosebank — fades, classic cuts, beard sculpting and the full hot-towel ritual — for regulars who book by name and walk-ins who become regulars.
