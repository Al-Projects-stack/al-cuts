Terms and Conditions

Last updated: 24 September 2026.

1. Acceptance

By booking a chair at AL CUTS (“the shop”, “we”), whether online, by phone or in person, you accept these terms. If you do not agree, please do not book.

2. Bookings and confirmation

An online booking is a request until we confirm it. We confirm by phone, SMS, WhatsApp or email, usually within one working day. Your slot is only guaranteed once confirmed. Details you provide (service, barber, date, time, contact) must be accurate.

3. Arrival and lateness policy

Please arrive 5 minutes early. A 15-minute grace period applies from your booked start time. After 15 minutes we may shorten the service to fit the chair schedule, or release the chair and treat the booking as a no-show.

4. Cancellation and rescheduling

Life happens. Cancel or reschedule free of charge with at least 24 hours’ notice by calling 011 555 0142. Rescheduling inside 24 hours is subject to chair availability on the day.

5. No-shows

Clients who repeatedly fail to arrive without notice may be asked to confirm future bookings by phone, or may be refused online booking. Be kind to the chair — someone else wants it.

6. Pricing and payment

All prices are in South African rand (R) and include VAT where applicable. Payment is due at the chair by card or cash. The price list on the Services page is the single source of truth; any difference at the shop will be resolved in your favour.

7. First-visit offer conditions

First-time clients who book online receive R30 off one service, applied at the chair. One redemption per person, not transferable, not redeemable for cash, and cannot be combined with other promotions unless stated.

8. Health, skin conditions and patch disclosure

Tell your barber before the cut about any scalp or skin condition, allergy (including to nickel, latex or fragrances), recent injury, or medical treatment affecting skin or hair. We may decline or adapt a service — including hot-towel shaves — where proceeding could harm you. A patch or sensitivity disclosure may be required before chemical-adjacent products are used.

9. Minors and kids cuts

Kids cuts are for children under 12. A parent or guardian must be present in the shop for the full service and must consent to the cut on the child’s behalf.

10. Liability

We exercise professional skill and care, sanitise tools after every client, and use a fresh blade per shave. To the maximum extent permitted by South African law, our liability for any claim arising from a service is limited to the price paid for that service. Nothing here limits liability that cannot legally be limited.

11. Photography and social media

We love showing sharp work. We will only photograph you or post your cut on our social channels with your clear permission, given at the shop. You may withdraw that permission at any time by contacting us.

12. Personal data

We process booking details under South Africa’s Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). See our Privacy notice for what we collect, why, how long we keep it, and how to request deletion.

13. Changes to these terms

We may update these terms from time to time. The version in force is the one published on this page at the time you book.

14. Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of the Republic of South Africa. The courts of Johannesburg have jurisdiction over any dispute.
