Bookings

Online bookings are requests until confirmed by phone. Arriving more than 15 minutes late may require rebooking. Free rebooking is available up to 24 hours before your slot.

Prices and payment

All prices are in South African rand and payable at the chair by card or cash. The first-visit offer (R30 off) applies once per customer to bookings made online.

House rules

  • Children under 12 must be accompanied by an adult.
  • Walk-ins are welcome when chairs are free; bookings take priority.
  • Tools are sanitised after every client and a fresh blade is used per shave.
